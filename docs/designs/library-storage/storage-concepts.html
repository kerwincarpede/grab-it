Library storage: three directions

Sample data: 1,284 items across screenshots, GIFs and videos. Change the “Keep” settings in any option to preview what cleanup would remove (shown hatched). “Clean up” is simulated and offers Undo.

A
Ring: a storage pill in the title bar that opens a popover Out of the way until you want it. The pill shows the total with a tiny ring, and clicking it shows a donut by type, how much of the disk it uses, and the Keep settings. Small to build; it doesn't change the grid.
