## All-in-one scrolling capture

Choose **Scroll** after Record in All-in-one, drag an area or click a window, then confirm **Start scrolling**. Scroll slowly by hand vertically or horizontally, keeping some content visible between movements. Quick Access shows the image size: **Done** saves the stitched image and **Cancel** discards it. Capture pauses at 30,000 px until you choose Done or Cancel. Keep the content still apart from scrolling.

All-in-one number keys are **1–8**: Region, Window, Screen, Text, Colour, Record, Scroll and Timer.
