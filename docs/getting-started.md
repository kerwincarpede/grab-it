## Quick Access

After a capture, drag the Quick Access thumbnail into a chat, email, document,
file manager or upload box to share the original PNG. A short click still opens
the editor; the dragged file does not include annotations or backdrops.

For a recording, drag its filename in the card's details area to share the MP4
or GIF. The preview still plays it when clicked. The card stays up during a drag
and after dropping or cancelling, then starts its dismiss timer again.
