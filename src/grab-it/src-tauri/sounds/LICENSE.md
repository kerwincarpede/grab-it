# Holga shutter sound

`2-holga-two-part.wav` is the approved edit of a Holga 120S shutter
recorded by Doitashimashite on 19 September 2006. The author dedicated
the recording to the public domain and permits unrestricted use where
dedication is unavailable, subject to local law. [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Holga_shuttersound.ogg)

The edit joins 0.295–0.425 s and 0.790–0.895 s from the source with
25 ms of silence, 3 ms edge fades and a peak level of −14 dBFS.
Grab-it bundles the 25,004-byte, 48 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV from the
approved attachment. [Archived attachment](https://github.com/kerwincarpede/grab-it/issues/325#issuecomment-7075826048)
