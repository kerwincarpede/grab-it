Click the Grab-it icon in the menu bar or tray for all of these. On Windows and macOS the click opens a small panel with a button for each capture, your latest captures, and links to the Grab-it window and Settings; right-click for the full menu. On Linux the click opens the menu.

## 📌 Pin

Float a capture above everything else as a reference while you work. Drag it around, scroll (or press `+`/`-`) to resize, double-click or `Esc` to close.

## 🔤 Capture text

Drag over anything on screen, even text in an image or video, and the text is copied, ready to paste. With [AI](/grab-it/ai/descriptions/) on, it's then tidied up: misread characters are fixed, and JSON, code and tables come out properly laid out. Drag over a QR code or barcode and its contents are copied instead (see below).

## 🔳 Scan a code

Use **Capture text** on a QR code or barcode, no phone needed. Its contents are copied, one per line when there are several, and the card shows what it found. **Copy text** copies the words around the code instead, and **Open link** opens a web link in your browser. Grab-it never opens a link unless you click.

## 🎨 Pick a colour

Click any pixel to copy its colour as a hex code like `#4C8DFF`.

## ⏱️ Timed capture

Wait 3, 5 or 10 seconds before capturing, so you have time to open a menu or hover a tooltip.

## 🔁 Repeat last region

Capture the exact same area again, without dragging.

## 🖥️ Capture screen / Monitor

Grab every monitor at once, or just one.

## 🧰 All-in-one

One shortcut for everything: the screen freezes with a toolbar at the top for **Region**, **Window**, **Screen**, **Text**, **Colour**, **Record** and a **Timer** (off, 3, 5 or 10 seconds). Click one or press its number, `1` to `7`. Screen captures straight away; with the Timer on, the overlay closes and Grab-it captures the area you picked once the time is up.

A region you drag stays selected so you can adjust it:

- drag a handle on a corner or edge to resize it, or drag inside it to move it,
- type an exact size such as 1280 × 720 into the width and height beside it (in the pixels the capture will have),
- or lock its shape with **16:9**, **4:3** or **1:1** (**Free** unlocks it).

Press `Enter` or the button beside it to capture or copy the text.

**Record** shows the recording setup toolbar under the mode toolbar, where you pick a region, a window or a screen and the recording options, then press **Record** or `Enter`; press the All-in-one shortcut again to stop the recording. See [Record your screen](/grab-it/recording/record/).

Your last region starts out selected on its screen; drag anywhere else for a new one, or click away to pick a window instead. On Wayland your desktop's own picker shows instead, as for a region.
