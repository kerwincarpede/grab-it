## Every monitor

**Capture screen (all monitors)** takes everything on every monitor at once, with no overlay and nothing to drag. Press `Ctrl + PrintScreen` (on macOS `⌘ ⇧ F`), or pick it in the tray menu.

With more than one monitor, the monitors are joined into one image laid out the way they're arranged in your display settings. Any gaps between monitors of different sizes stay transparent. The image uses your sharpest monitor's resolution, so no monitor loses detail; the others are scaled up to match.

## One monitor

The tray menu's **Monitor** submenu lists each monitor by number, name and size, such as *1. DELL U2720Q (3840×2160, primary)*. Pick one to capture just that monitor, straight away.

On Wayland, where Grab-it may not be able to list your monitors, the submenu shows **Choose a monitor…** instead, and your desktop asks which one to capture.

## From All-in-one

**Screen** in the [All-in-one](/grab-it/capture/all-in-one/) toolbar (key `3`) captures every monitor as it was frozen, the moment you pick it.

Want to wait a moment first? **Timed capture → Screen in 5 seconds** in the tray menu does just that. See [Timed capture](/grab-it/capture/timed/).
