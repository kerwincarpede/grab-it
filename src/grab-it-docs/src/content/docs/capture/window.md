title: Window
description: Hover a window and click it to capture just that window.

**Capture window** captures one window, cut out neatly at its edges. Press `Alt + PrintScreen` (on macOS `⌘ ⇧ W`), or pick **Capture window** in the tray menu.

1. The screen freezes, and the hint reads *"Click a window to capture it. Esc cancels."*
2. Move the pointer over the window you want. It's highlighted, with its title beside it.
3. Click. That window is captured.

`Esc` or right-click cancels.

The window is captured **as it looked when the screen froze**, so an open menu or a hover state stays in the picture. A window that spills over onto another monitor is captured on its own instead, so no part of it is cut off.

:::tip
You don't need this shortcut to capture a window: [Capture region](/grab-it/capture/region/) captures a window too when you click instead of dragging. **Capture window** only clicks, so a slip of the mouse never turns into a drag.
:::

On Wayland the desktop's own picker asks which window to capture. See [Wayland](/grab-it/capture/wayland/).
