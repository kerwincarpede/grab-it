title: Pick a colour
description: Click any pixel on screen to copy its colour as a hex code.

**Pick a colour** copies the colour of any pixel on your screen, in the `#RRGGBB` form design tools use. Press `Ctrl + Shift + PrintScreen` (on macOS `⌘ ⇧ 9`), or pick **Pick a colour** in the tray menu.

1. The screen freezes, and the hint reads *"Click to copy the colour under the cursor."*
2. Move the pointer over the colour you want. The magnifier shows the pixels around it, enlarged, with the colour of the one in the middle and its hex code underneath.
3. Click. The colour, such as `#4C8DFF`, is on your clipboard.

`Esc` or right-click cancels. Nothing is saved.

**Colour** in the [Capture & record…](/grab-it/capture/all-in-one/) toolbar (key `5`) does the same.

On Wayland, your desktop's own colour picker shows instead. See [Wayland](/grab-it/capture/wayland/).
