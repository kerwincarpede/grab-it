Grab-it sits in your system tray (menu bar on macOS) and captures with one key. Every capture is copied to your clipboard, saved as a PNG in `Pictures/Grab-it`, and shown on a Quick Access card.

- [Your first capture](/grab-it/capture/first-capture/): capture a region or a window, and what the Quick Access card offers.
- [More tricks](/grab-it/capture/more-tricks/): pins, text, codes, colours, timed captures, whole screens and All-in-one.

To record video instead, see [Recording](/grab-it/recording/).
