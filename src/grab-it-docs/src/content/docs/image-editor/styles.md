The left of the top bar shows the style for the tool you're using. Change it with nothing selected and it applies to what you draw next; change it with a shape selected and that shape changes too.

## Colour

Seven swatches, in order: red, orange, green, blue, purple, black and white. Press `1` to `7` to pick one from the keyboard. The last swatch opens a colour picker for any other colour.

Blur, Spotlight and Crop have no colour. With Crop, the swatches make way for its [ratios](/grab-it/image-editor/crop/#ratios).

## Width and size

The button after the colours sets how big the tool draws. Click it for a preview and a slider, or change it without looking:

- `[` makes it one step smaller, `]` one step bigger.
- `Alt/⌥ + scroll` over the screenshot does the same: scroll up for bigger.

| For | It's called | Steps (pixels) | To start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrow, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse, Freehand, Highlighter | **Width** | 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 | 4 |
| Text, Callout | **Text size** | 12, 16, 20, 24, 30, 36, 48, 64, 96 | 30 |
| Step | **Badge size** | 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 30, 36, 48 | 20 |

**Each tool keeps its own size.** A thick highlighter doesn't make your arrows thick. The [highlighter](/grab-it/image-editor/highlighter/) is always drawn wider than its width, to cover a line of text.

## Options

Some tools have one more option in the top bar:

| Tool | Option | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rectangle, Ellipse | **Fill** | Fills the shape with its colour, see-through |
| Text | **Plain**, **Pill**, **Outline**, **Shadow** | How the text is drawn; see [Text styles](/grab-it/image-editor/text/#text-styles) |
| Callout | **Text background** | Fills the bubble with its colour instead of white |
| Blur | **Pixelate** / **Smooth** | Blocks of colour, or a soft blur |
| Crop | **Free**, **Original**, **16:9**, **4:3**, **1:1**, **9:16** | The crop's shape |

## Remembered for next time

The editor remembers your tool, colour, each tool's size, **Fill**, the text style, the blur style and your last backdrop, and opens with them the next time, in any capture.
