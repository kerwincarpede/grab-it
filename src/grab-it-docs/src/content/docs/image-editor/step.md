**Key: `N`**

Click to drop a numbered badge. The first is **1**, the next **2**, and so on. Delete one and the rest renumber themselves, so the steps always count up in the order you added them.

![Numbered steps 1, 2 and 3 on three cards.](../../../assets/image-editor/step.png)

**Options:** colour, and a **Badge size** (10 to 48, 20 to start with; also `[` and `]`). Each badge is a filled circle of the colour with a white rim and a white number.

A badge stays selected once dropped, so you can drag it into place. It resizes from its corners, and stays upright when you turn the screenshot.
