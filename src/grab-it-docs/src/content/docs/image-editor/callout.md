**Key: `K`**

1. Press on the thing you want to point at.
2. Drag to where the note should go, and let go. A text box opens there.
3. Type, then press `Enter`, or click somewhere else, to place it. `Esc` cancels.

A plain click, without dragging, puts the note where you clicked, with its tail pointing down and to the left.

The new callout stays selected. Drag the round handle on the tail's tip to point it somewhere else. To change the text later, **double-click** it. Clearing all of it deletes the callout.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Colour | The bubble's outline and the text, or the whole bubble when **Text background** is on |
| **Text size** | 12 to 96 pixels, 30 to start with; also `[` and `]` |
| **Text background** | Fills the bubble with its colour, with the text in white or near-black, whichever reads better. Off, the bubble is white with a coloured outline |

The bubble and its tail are one shape: moving, nudging or duplicating a callout takes the tail with it. A selected callout resizes from its corners, keeping its shape, and the tail's tip stays where it points. When you've turned the screenshot, the text stays upright and the tail still points at the same spot.
