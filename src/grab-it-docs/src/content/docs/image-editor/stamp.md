**Key: `I`**

Pick a stamp from the stamp button in the top bar, then click the screenshot to drop it there, centred on the pointer. It's quicker than a text label for marking something as right, wrong or important.

The picker has four sections:

- **Icons**: check mark, cross, warning, question mark and star.
- **Pointers**: an arrow pointer, a pointing hand and a text pointer, for showing where to click.
- **Keys**: a keycap with a label you type, and ready-made keys: `Ctrl`, `Shift`, `Alt`, `⌘`, `⌥`, `Enter`, `Esc`, `Tab`, `Space`, `←`, `→`, `↑` and `↓`.
- **Emoji**: 30 common emoji, such as 👍, 🎉, 🔥, 💡 and 🐛.

**Options:** colour for icons and keys, and a **Stamp size** (24 to 160, 48 to start with; also `[` and `]`). Pointers and emoji keep their own colours, so the colours are hidden for them. The editor remembers the stamp you picked last.

## Keys

Pick **Blank key** and click: a text box opens where the key goes. Type its label and press `Enter`. A key grows wider for a longer label, and its label is drawn in white or black, whichever reads better on the key's colour. Double-click a key to change its label; clear the label to remove the key.

## Move, resize and turn

A stamp stays selected once dropped, so you can drag it into place.

- **Drag a corner handle** to resize it. It keeps its shape.
- **Drag the round handle above it** to turn it. Hold `Shift` to turn in 15° steps.
- **Pick another stamp** while one is selected to swap it, keeping its size and angle.

Duplicate, delete, nudge and undo work as for any shape (see [Select](/grab-it/image-editor/select/)). Stamps stay upright and never show mirrored when you turn or flip the screenshot, and copies and exports include them.
