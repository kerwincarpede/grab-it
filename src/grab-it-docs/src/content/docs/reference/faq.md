## Is Grab-it free?

Yes. Grab-it is free and open source, with no account and no subscription.

## Does Grab-it need the internet?

No. Capturing, editing, recording, reading text in images and searching all work offline. Grab-it goes online only to check for updates, to download ffmpeg the first time it needs it, and to describe captures if you use a cloud AI. See [Privacy](/grab-it/reference/privacy/).

## Where are my captures?

In your save folder, `Pictures/Grab-it` by default, as ordinary PNG files. Recordings are in its `recordings` folder. Click **Show in folder** in the Library to go straight to one. Change the folder in [Settings → General](/grab-it/reference/settings/#general).

## Can I edit a capture again later?

Yes. Your annotations are kept beside the capture, so opening it from the Library brings them back, still editable.

## Do I need AI?

No. Reading text in images, search and Capture text all work without it. AI adds descriptions and categories, and tidies up captured text. See [AI](/grab-it/ai/).

## Can I keep everything on my computer and still use AI?

Yes. Run a model with [Ollama](https://ollama.com) and pick the **Ollama** preset in **Settings → AI integration**. See [Providers](/grab-it/ai/providers/#run-a-model-on-your-computer-with-ollama).

## How do I record my screen?

Pick **Record** from the tray menu or Capture & record…. See [Record your screen](/grab-it/recording/record/).

## Can I script Grab-it?

Yes, with `--open`. See [Command line](/grab-it/reference/command-line/).

## How do I update?

Grab-it checks for updates by itself and the Grab-it window offers to install them. See [Updates](/grab-it/get-started/updates/).
