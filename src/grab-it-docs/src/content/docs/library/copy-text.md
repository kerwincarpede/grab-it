Grab-it reads the text in every screenshot you take, so you can copy it later without going back to the original.

## Copy all of it

- Hover a tile and click its **Copy text** button (a "T" in a frame).
- Or open the details and click the same button beside **Pin**, or **Copy** next to **Text in image**.

The text goes to your clipboard and the Library says how much, such as *Copied 4 lines of text*.

## Copy part of it

The details panel shows everything Grab-it read under **Text in image**. Select just the part you want and copy it as you normally would (`Ctrl + C`, or `⌘ C` on a Mac).

## When the text isn't there yet

- *Not read yet. Copy reads it now.* Grab-it hasn't got to this one in the background yet; **Copy** reads it straight away.
- *The text could not be read. Copy tries again.*
- *No text found in this image.*

:::tip
This is different from [Capture text](/grab-it/capture/more-tricks/), which copies the text in an area of the screen right now. Copy text works on captures you already have, including full-screen and window captures, and on the values of any QR codes or barcodes in them.
:::
