Click a tile to open its **details** on the right. The arrow keys move between tiles while the grid has focus. Click **Previous capture** or **Next capture** at the top of the details pane to step through the grid and focus the pane. With focus on the pane, press `←` or `↑` for the previous capture and `→` or `↓` for the next. When a field has focus, the arrows move within that field. Click **Close details** or press `Esc` to close the pane. Drag its left edge to resize it, or double-click the edge to reset it.

## What the details show

- A preview. Double-click it to open the capture in the editor, or to play a recording.
- The window title (or file name), the app and when you took it.
- Buttons for the capture: **Edit**, **Copy**, **Copy text**, **Pin** and **Show in folder**. A recording has **Edit** (MP4s only), **Play** and **Show in folder**.
- **File**, **Window**, **Size** (in pixels), and for a recording its **Length** and **File size**. With AI on, the **Category** too.
- **Text in image**: everything Grab-it read. See [Copy text later](/grab-it/library/copy-text/).
- **Description**: the AI description. **Redo** describes it again; **Clear** removes the description, category and AI tags.

## Tags

Type in **Tags** and press `Enter` to add one. Type a comma to add several at once: `bug, login` adds two. Press `Backspace` in the empty box to remove the last tag, or click the **×** on any tag. Tags save as you add them.

Tags the AI added are marked with **✦** (*Added by AI*). Remove any you don't want with their **×**; the rest stay.

Every tag gets its own colour, the same everywhere it appears. Your most used tags show in the sidebar, and every tag is offered in **Filters → Tag**.

## Notes

Type anything in **Notes**: why you took it, the ticket it belongs to, what to do next. It saves when you click away. Search looks in notes too.

## Delete

**Delete…** at the bottom asks *Delete this capture and its annotations?* (or *Delete this recording and its edits?*). **Delete permanently** removes the file from your disk along with its edits, tags and notes. It can't be undone.

## Where captures live

Captures are files in your [save folder](/grab-it/reference/settings/#general), `Pictures/Grab-it` by default, so you can open them in any app. The Library scans `originals` and `recordings`, including their month folders, and shows the files together by date. Changing the save folder leaves earlier captures where they are, and they stay in your Library.
