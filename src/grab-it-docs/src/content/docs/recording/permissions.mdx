Recording uses your screen and, depending on the [options](/grab-it/recording/options/) you pick, your microphone, your camera and your mouse and keyboard. Only macOS asks for permission first; Windows and Linux record straight away.

<Tabs syncKey="os">
<TabItem label="Windows" icon="seti:windows">

Grab-it needs no permission to record. The webcam bubble may use your camera without asking, as it's Grab-it's own window.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="macOS" icon="apple">

macOS asks once for each of these, the first time it's needed:

| Permission | Asked for when | Without it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screen Recording** | You first capture or record. | Grab-it can't record. It offers to open System Settings. |
| **Microphone** | You first record an MP4 with a microphone. The recording waits for your answer. | The recording is saved without your voice, and the card says why. |
| **Camera** | You first turn on **Webcam**. | The bubble can't show your camera. |
| **Input Monitoring** | You first record with **Clicks** or **Keys** on. | The recording is saved without clicks or keystrokes, and the card says why. |

System sound needs no permission, but it needs macOS 13 or later.

If you said **Don't Allow** earlier, Grab-it shows a message with **Open System Settings** the next time. To turn a permission on yourself:

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
2. Choose **Screen Recording**, **Microphone**, **Camera** or **Input Monitoring**.
3. Turn on **Grab-it**.
4. For Screen Recording and Input Monitoring, quit and reopen Grab-it if macOS asks you to.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux" icon="linux">

On X11, Grab-it needs no permission to record, and the webcam bubble may use your camera without asking.

Under Wayland, recording isn't available: only your desktop may read the screen there, and its screenshot picker can't record. See [Troubleshooting](/grab-it/reference/troubleshooting/#on-linux-wayland-the-desktop-asks-me-to-pick-an-area-itself).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::note
Updating from an older version can make macOS treat Grab-it as a new app, once. If recording stops working after an update, turn Grab-it off and on again under **Screen Recording**. See [Troubleshooting](/grab-it/reference/troubleshooting/).
:::
