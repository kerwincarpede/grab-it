Grab-it records a region, a window or a whole display as an MP4 or an animated GIF. An MP4 can carry your microphone and the sound your computer plays; either format can show your webcam, your clicks and the keys you press.

- [Record your screen](/grab-it/recording/record/): start a recording, pick what to record, and stop it.
- [Recording options](/grab-it/recording/options/): format, microphone, system sound, webcam, clicks, keys and the countdown.
- [The recording toolbar](/grab-it/recording/toolbar/): mute, pause, stop, restart or discard while you record, and move the toolbar out of the way.
- [Permissions](/grab-it/recording/permissions/): what macOS asks for, and what to do when you said no.

Recordings are saved under `recordings` in your save folder, named after the moment you started, like `2026-03-07 09-05-01.mp4`. In [Settings → General](/grab-it/reference/settings/#general), you can put new recordings in month folders and add the selected app's name to window recordings. When a recording stops, the Quick Access card shows it with its length and size. To trim it, zoom in and put it on a backdrop, see the [Video editor](/grab-it/video-editor/).

:::note
On Linux under Wayland, recording isn't available: only your desktop may read the screen there. See [Troubleshooting](/grab-it/reference/troubleshooting/#on-linux-wayland-the-desktop-asks-me-to-pick-an-area-itself).
:::
